# Amar Dentist Privacy Notice — Pilot Draft

Status: product-testing draft only. Replace after Bangladesh legal, clinical, payment-provider, and app-store review. Effective date: to be set before the closed pilot.

## What the service handles

Amar Dentist handles account and contact information, patient and dependent profiles, appointment and clinic messages, consent choices, payment references, and dental records created by authorized clinicians. With permission, location is used only to rank nearby clinics. The app does not require background location.

Clinical photographs, X-rays, prescriptions, and medical or allergy information are private health information. They are stored in private storage and are available only through server-enforced permissions. A clinic can view another clinic's finalized history only when the patient has granted active consent for that clinic. Revoking consent blocks future cross-clinic access but does not erase lawful records already created for care, safety, audit, or regulatory needs.

## AI assistance

AI may help a verified treating dentist prepare a private draft. The dentist must review each required field before accepting it, and only the dentist can finalize a clinical record or prescription. Experimental X-ray observations are separately labelled and are not a diagnosis.

Patients may use AI to organize symptoms, receive general information, understand finalized records, and reach booking. Patient AI does not diagnose or prescribe. Raw provider output is not shown to patients. AI tasks retain the prompt version, provider and model, bounded input, raw output, reviewed changes, final output, reviewer, time, usage, and estimated cost for safety and accountability.

## Sharing and processors

Information may be processed by contracted infrastructure, notification, payment, subscription, and AI providers only as needed to deliver the service. Provider contracts, data locations, retention terms, and international transfer safeguards must be confirmed before production. Amar Dentist does not sell patient information.

## Retention, security, and rights

Access decisions are enforced on the server and important actions are audited. Provider keys and payment secrets remain server-side. Monitoring must exclude clinical text and images. Backups remain protected.

People may request access, correction, export, or deletion where permitted. Some appointment, financial, consent, prescription, and clinical records may need to be retained for care, fraud prevention, audit, or law. Production contact details, retention periods, complaint routing, and the responsible legal entity must be inserted before pilot acceptance.

## Emergency boundary

Amar Dentist is not an emergency service. Trouble breathing, rapidly increasing facial swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, major facial injury, or other severe symptoms require immediate local emergency help.

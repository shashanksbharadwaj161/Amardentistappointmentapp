# Amar Dentist Terms — Pilot Draft

Status: product-testing draft only. Replace after Bangladesh legal, clinical, payment-provider, and app-store review.

Amar Dentist is a booking, clinic-operations, record, messaging, payment, and decision-support platform. It does not itself provide dental treatment. Clinics and professionals are responsible for their services, professional registrations, clinical decisions, prices, availability, and legal duties.

Users must provide accurate information, protect their account, and use the platform lawfully. Clinical and payment workflows must not be used to mislead, impersonate another person, interfere with security, scrape private data, or submit harmful content.

AI output is assistance, not final care. Patient AI cannot diagnose or prescribe. Dentist AI creates private drafts that require field-by-field professional review. An AI draft must never replace examination, clinical judgment, appropriate imaging, referral, or emergency care.

The current appointment policy is: cancellation or rescheduling at least 24 hours before the visit makes the deposit refundable or transferable; under 24 hours, the deposit is forfeited; after a 15-minute no-show threshold, the deposit is forfeited and the already-started slot does not reopen. A future reopened slot may be reserved for the first eligible waitlisted patient for 15 minutes. The production terms must align these rules with provider agreements and consumer law.

Mock payments, sandbox subscriptions, demo clinics, and fictional profiles must be clearly identified during testing. A public launch requires approved payment-provider accounts, production store accounts, final legal documents, privacy contacts, refund routing, and practicing-dentist validation.

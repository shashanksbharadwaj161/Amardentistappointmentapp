# Administration console surface brief

- **Mode:** Operate. Platform staff resolve verification and operational exceptions accurately.
- **Audience and job:** Super Admins and invited Admins review evidence, approve providers, investigate failures, and manage configuration within their permissions.
- **Primary proof:** Credential facts, immutable decision history, masked secrets, and clear consequences before privileged actions.
- **Direction:** The Guided Care Ledger translated into a dense desktop workbench with a navy rail, pearl work field, queue, and evidence inspector.
- **Memorable moment:** Selecting a verification row opens its evidence alongside the queue, preserving place and making approval provenance obvious.
- **Constraints:** Keyboard-first, WCAG 2.2 AA, tablet adaptation, no routine clinical content for Admins, destructive actions require confirmation and audit reason.

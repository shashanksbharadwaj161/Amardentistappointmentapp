# Mobile application surface brief

- **Mode:** Operate. Patients complete a trusted care journey; professionals run time-sensitive clinic work.
- **Audience and job:** Bangladesh patients find verified care and retain records; clinic users move from schedule to encounter without losing context.
- **Primary proof:** Real availability, explicit verification, server-confirmed booking state, and named clinical authorship.
- **Direction:** The Guided Care Ledger. Patient discovery moves from map night into pearl booking surfaces; professional work follows a continuous day spine into the patient record.
- **Memorable moment:** A selected map route resolves into a held appointment and then a verified QR confirmation without changing interaction vocabulary.
- **Constraints:** Native iOS/Android navigation, bilingual English/Bangla, reduced motion, screen readers, unreliable networks, no fabricated clinic content.
